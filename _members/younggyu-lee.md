Younggyu Lee is a postgraduate student in the MSc Cognitive Neuroimaging and Data Science (Computational Neuroscience). Under the Supervision of Dr. Hyojin Park at the Centre for Human Brain Health (CHBH) of the University of Birmingham. His main project focuses on non-invasive decoding of imagined speech from the brain activity using MEG. In addition to the project, he is conducting various research on speech decoding. Before the current study, he completed his bachelor’s degree at Konkuk University in Software, and has been working in the BCI field for more than three years.
